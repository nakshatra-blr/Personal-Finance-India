## ✅ First Salary Checklist

```
Month 1:
☐ Open a Savings Account (high-interest: Kotak/IDFC/Fi)
☐ Get a Credit Card (build credit history early)
☐ Start Emergency Fund (put 20% of salary)
☐ Get Health Insurance (don't rely on employer only)
☐ Start 1 SIP (even Rs.500/month counts)
☐ File for PAN card if not done
☐ Link Aadhaar to bank, PAN, phone

Month 3:
☐ Emergency fund = 1 month salary ✓
☐ Increase SIP amount
☐ Get Term Insurance (if family depends on you)
☐ Start PPF (Rs.500/month minimum)

Month 6:
☐ Emergency fund = 3 months expenses ✓
☐ Review and increase investments
☐ Plan tax-saving before March rush
```

## 📊 50-30-20 Budgeting Rule

| Category | % of Salary | Includes |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Needs** | 50% | Rent, food, transport, EMIs, bills, insurance |
| **Wants** | 30% | Shopping, eating out, entertainment, subscriptions |
| **Savings** | 20% | Investments, SIPs, emergency fund, PPF |

### Example: Rs.30,000 salary

| Category | Amount | Breakdown |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Needs (50%) | Rs.15,000 | Rent: 8K + Food: 4K + Transport: 2K + Bills: 1K |
| Wants (30%) | Rs.9,000 | Shopping, movies, dining out |
| Savings (20%) | Rs.6,000 | SIP: 3K + Emergency: 2K + PPF: 1K |

**Pro tip:** If you can do 30-20-50 (50% savings) in your early years while living with parents — you'll be way ahead.

## 🏦 Emergency Fund

### How Much?
```
Single, living with parents:  3 months expenses
Single, living alone:         6 months expenses
Married, single income:       9 months expenses
Freelancer/business owner:    12 months expenses
```

### Where to Keep It?
| Option | Returns | Liquidity | Best For |
|--------|---------|-----------|----------|
| Savings Account | 3-6% | Instant | First Rs.50K |
| Liquid Mutual Fund | 6-7% | 1 day | Rs.50K - 3L |
| FD (short-term) | 7-8% | Penalty for early withdrawal | Not ideal |

**Rule:** Emergency fund should be BORING — don't chase returns here. The goal is instant access.

## 📈 Where to Invest Your Money

### Investment Pyramid (Bottom = Start Here)

```
         /\\
        /  \\      STOCKS (Direct equity - only after learning)
       /    \\
      /______\\    ELSS / Mid-cap MF (slightly higher risk)
     /        \\
    /__________\\  INDEX FUNDS / Large-cap MF (moderate risk)
   /            \\
  /______________\\ PPF + EPF + FD (safe, guaranteed)
```

## 💹 SIP & Mutual Funds

### What is SIP?
Systematic Investment Plan — invest a fixed amount monthly into a mutual fund. Like a recurring deposit, but in the stock market.

### SIP of Rs.5,000/month can grow to:

| Duration | Investment | Value (12% return) | Wealth Created |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|----------------|
| 5 years | Rs.3,00,000 | Rs.4,12,000 | Rs.1,12,000 |
| 10 years | Rs.6,00,000 | Rs.11,62,000 | Rs.5,62,000 |
| 20 years | Rs.12,00,000 | Rs.49,96,000 | Rs.37,96,000 |
| 30 years | Rs.18,00,000 | Rs.1,76,50,000 | Rs.1,58,50,000 |

**Rs.5,000/month → Rs.1.76 CRORE in 30 years!** That's the power of compounding.

### Which Fund to Start With?

| Type | Risk | When to Choose | Example |
|------|------|----------------|---------|
| **Nifty 50 Index Fund** | Medium | First SIP ever | UTI Nifty 50 |
| **Flexi-cap Fund** | Medium | 5+ year horizon | Parag Parikh Flexi Cap |
| **ELSS** | Medium | Need tax saving | Mirae Asset Tax Saver |
| **Liquid Fund** | Low | Emergency fund parking | Quantum Liquid |

### Where to Invest?
- **Groww** — Beginner friendly
- **Zerodha Coin** — Direct funds, low cost

## 🛡️ Insurance (Must-Haves)

### Health Insurance
```
Must Have: YES (even if employer provides one)
Cover: Minimum Rs.5-10 lakhs (individual)
When: Get it in your 20s (lowest premium)
Top picks: Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Care Health
Tax benefit: Section 80D (Rs.25,000 deduction)
```

### Term Insurance (if anyone depends on your income)
```
Must Have: YES (if married / parents depend on you)
Cover: 10-15x your annual income
When: As soon as you have dependents
Cost: Rs.500-700/month for Rs.1 Cr cover (age 25)
Top picks: ICICI Pru iProtect, HDFC Click2Protect, LIC Tech Term
```

### DON'T Buy
```
❌ ULIPs (high charges, low returns)
❌ Endowment plans (returns barely beat FD)
❌ Money-back policies (waste of money)
❌ LIC Jeevan [anything] (except Term Plan)

Rule: Keep insurance and investment SEPARATE
```

## 📊 Credit Score

### What is CIBIL Score?
A 3-digit number (300-900) that tells banks how trustworthy you are with credit.

| Score Range | Rating | Loan Approval |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| 750-900 | Excellent | Easy approval, best rates |
| 700-749 | Good | Approval likely |
| 650-699 | Average | May face higher rates |
| Below 650 | Poor | Difficult to get loans |

### How to Build Good Credit (for students/freshers)
```
1. Get a credit card (secured card if needed)
2. Use only 30% of credit limit
3. ALWAYS pay full bill before due date
4. Never miss a payment (set auto-pay)
5. Don't apply for multiple cards at once
6. Keep old cards active (credit history length matters)
```

### Check Score Free
- CIBIL: myscore.cibil.com
- Groww / Paytm / PhonePe (in-app)
- BankBazaar

## 🧓 Retirement Planning

### The Math (why starting early wins)

**Person A:** Starts SIP at age 25, invests till 55 (30 years)
**Person B:** Starts SIP at age 35, invests till 55 (20 years)

Both invest Rs.10,000/month at 12% returns:

| | Person A (start: 25) | Person B (start: 35) |
|---|---|---|
| Total invested | Rs.36 lakhs | Rs.24 lakhs |
| Corpus at 55 | Rs.3.53 Crore | Rs.99 Lakhs |
| Extra wealth | **Rs.2.54 Crore more!** | — |

**Starting 10 years earlier = 3.5x more money** with only 50% more investment.

## ❌ Common Money Mistakes

```
1. ❌ "I'll invest when I earn more" — START NOW, even Rs.500
2. ❌ Buying insurance as investment (LIC endowment plans)
3. ❌ No health insurance (one hospital visit = savings wiped)
4. ❌ Lifestyle inflation (spending more as salary increases)
5. ❌ No emergency fund (forced to break investments)
6. ❌ Following stock tips on WhatsApp/Telegram
7. ❌ Taking personal loans for wants (not needs)
8. ❌ Not filing ITR (miss out on refunds + affects visa/loans)
9. ❌ Ignoring EPF/PPF (free tax savings!)
10. ❌ Waiting for "right time" to invest (time IN market > timing the market)
```
